Questions

Is this the official FAA test?

No. Sourced 107 is an independent study aid. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the FAA or PSI. The real test is taken at an FAA-approved testing center.

Does it cover the new chart questions?

Yes. Since October 2026 the test shows images from current FAA VFR charts on screen. The app includes practice questions in that format.

What if I think an answer is wrong?

Tap “Report this question” under the explanation. Every answer cites the FAA rule it comes from, so it can be checked quickly. Fixes arrive in an app update.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. There's no account, and everything works offline.

What does it cost?

It's free to try. Full Access unlocks every question, unlimited mock exams and every chart drill for $19.99, once. There's no subscription.
