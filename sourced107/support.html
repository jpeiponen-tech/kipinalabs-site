Sourced 107 support

Email support@kipinalabs.com. Replies usually within a few days.

I think an answer is wrong

Tap "Report this question" under the explanation. Every answer cites the FAA source it comes from, so it can be checked quickly. Fixes arrive in an app update.

Restore Full Access on a new iPhone

Open Settings in the app → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Is this the official FAA test?

No. Sourced 107 is an independent study aid. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the FAA or PSI. The real test is taken at an FAA-approved testing center.

Does it cover chart questions?

Yes. The app includes sectional chart practice with zoomable excerpts from the FAA testing supplement (FAA-CT-8080-2H).
