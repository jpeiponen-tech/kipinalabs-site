Sourced 107 privacy policy

Effective 24 October 2026

Sourced 107 is published by Kipina Labs LLC, a Georgia, USA company.

In short: Sourced 107 doesn't collect any data. Your progress stays on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

Your answers, scores, mock exam results, test date and settings are stored on your iPhone, to schedule reviews and show your readiness. Deleting the app deletes them.

Reporting a question

"Report this question" opens your email app with the question's ID filled in. What you send, and from which address, is up to you. Reports are used only to check and fix questions.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. The app only learns whether you've unlocked Full Access. It never sees your payment details.

Notifications

Study reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. They don't go through any server.

Children

Sourced 107 isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
