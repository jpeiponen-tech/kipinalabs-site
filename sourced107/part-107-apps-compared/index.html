Where other apps are stronger

  • Free: Part 107 Practice Test 2026 says it is "100% free" with no ads and no paywalls, and it has a 4.9 rating from 51 people. If price is all that matters, start there.
  • More questions: FAA Part 107 Drone Pilot Exam lists 649 original questions and costs $24.99 once. Remote Pilot Test Prep lists over 500. Sourced 107 has 330.
  • Track record: FAA PART 107 Practice Test and Remote Pilot Test Prep each have hundreds to thousands of ratings. Sourced 107 is new and has none yet.
  • Video lessons: Remote Pilot Test Prep and 107 Pilot School both sell a video course alongside their questions. Sourced 107 is practice questions only.
  • Cheapest paid app: 107 Pilot School and Prepware Remote Pilot UAS cost $4.99. Part 107 Drone Pilot Test Prep costs $19.99 once and is built around sectional charts and aviation weather, with a sectional chart trainer.

What Sourced 107 does differently

  • The source, word for word. Every answer shows the passage it's based on, quoted exactly from the regulation, the FAA study guide or the testing supplement, with where to find it. Sourced 107 shows the text itself, so you can check the answer without leaving the question.
  • Chart and weather report questions. 32 questions use figures from the FAA testing supplement (FAA-CT-8080-2H): sectional charts, METARs and TAFs.
  • One purchase. No subscription, no account, and it works offline.
  • Checked before release. Every question is built from its source text, and must pass automated checks and two automated reviews before it ships.

It's free to try: questions from every area and a mini mock exam, before you decide.
