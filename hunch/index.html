Sleep & recovery experiments

Your watch or ring already measures how you sleep and recover. Hunch helps you find out what actually changes it: test one habit for 14 nights and see if your numbers really move.

Coming soon to the App Store

For iPhone · Reads Apple Health · Your first completed experiment is free

One change, tested properly

Most health tips are guesses. Hunch turns one into a fair test, using the data your devices already collect.

Pick one change

No caffeine after noon, the same bedtime every night, eating earlier. Choose one, or start from a lead in Insights.

Live it for two weeks

Seven nights as usual, then seven with the change. Hunch reminds you and keeps count of the days you stuck to it.

See whether it seemed to help

Hunch compares the two weeks and shows whether your numbers moved more than day-to-day ups and downs would explain. One person over two weeks is a lead, not proof.

Everything at a glance

Today, at a glance

  • Recovery, training load, stress and steps, measured against your own normal
  • An effort range for today and a realistic bedtime for tonight
  • Home and lock screen widgets

Spot your patterns

  • Tag yesterday in one tap: alcohol, a late meal, a stressful day, travel
  • Insights show what goes with your shorter nights and lower recovery, with a test for many leads
  • Choose your focus: sleep, recovery or stress, and Hunch shows only what matters to you

Private by design

Everything is worked out on your iPhone. No account, no ads, no tracking. Hunch asks before it reads anything from Apple Health, and your health data stays on your iPhone unless you choose to share a report. Withdraw or delete it any time in Settings.
