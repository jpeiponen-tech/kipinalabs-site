Hunch privacy policy

Effective 25 October 2026

Hunch is published by Kipina Labs LLC, a Georgia, USA company.

In short: Hunch doesn't collect your data. Everything it reads and works out stays on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your experiments and check-ins are saved in Hunch's storage on your iPhone. If you back up your iPhone (to iCloud or a computer), that backup includes them, as it does for other apps; Kipina Labs can't access your backups.

Health data

Only after you agree on Hunch's consent screen, and allow each type in Apple's permission sheet, Hunch reads data from Apple Health, such as sleep, heart rate, heart rate variability, activity and nutrition. It uses that data to show trends, scores, targets and experiment results. All of this is worked out on your iPhone. Hunch has no servers, and it never sends your health data anywhere.

Hunch doesn't write anything to Apple Health.

You can withdraw your consent at any time in Settings. Hunch then stops reading Apple Health.

Hunch never uses health data for advertising, never sells it, and never shares it with anyone.

Other data on your iPhone

Your experiments, morning check-ins, tags, focus areas, settings and your health data consent (with its date) are stored on your iPhone. The home and lock screen widgets read a short summary (your recovery score, today's targets, the effort so far today and the time it was made), also stored on your iPhone. Hunch marks those numbers as private, so iOS can hide them while your iPhone is locked. Settings → Delete all my data removes all of it, and so does deleting the app. Data in Apple Health stays there until you delete it in the Health app.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple. Hunch only learns whether you have Hunch Pro. It never sees your payment details.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. They don't go through any server.

Health summary report

If you choose to make a health summary report, Hunch creates the PDF on your iPhone and opens the share sheet. It goes only where you send it, such as Mail, Messages or Files. Hunch doesn't send it anywhere itself, and Kipina Labs never receives it. Hunch deletes its own copy once you're done sharing.

Consumer health data

Kipina Labs doesn't collect, share or sell consumer health data. Hunch reads and processes your health data only on your iPhone; none of it reaches Kipina Labs or anyone else unless you share a report yourself. Our consumer health data privacy policy lists the health data Hunch handles on your iPhone and why, and how to confirm, access, delete or withdraw your consent.

Children

Hunch isn't directed at children under 13.

Not medical advice

Hunch is a wellness app, not a medical device. It doesn't diagnose or treat anything. Talk to your doctor before changing any treatment.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
