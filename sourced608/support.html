I think an answer is wrong

Email us the question and the answer you expected. Every answer shows the EPA rule or EPA page it quotes, so it can be checked quickly against the source. Fixes arrive in an app update.

Restore Full Access on a new iPhone

Tap any locked section or mock exam, then Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Is this the official EPA 608 test?

No. Sourced 608 is an independent study aid. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the EPA or any certifying organization, and it doesn't certify anyone. The real test is given by an EPA-approved certifying program.

What's free?

A Core drill set and the Core mock exam. Full Access is one purchase that unlocks Type I, II and III, their mock exams and the Universal mock. There's no subscription.

The study reminder doesn't arrive

Check that notifications are on for Sourced 608 in the Settings app. The app shows a note on Today when they're off.
