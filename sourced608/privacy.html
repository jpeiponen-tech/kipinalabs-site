Sourced 608 privacy policy

Effective 11 November 2026

Sourced 608 is published by Kipina Labs LLC, a Georgia, USA company.

In short: Sourced 608 doesn't collect any data. Your progress stays on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

Your answers, mock exam results and the certificate you're studying for are stored on your iPhone, to schedule reviews and show your practice estimate. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. The app only learns whether you've unlocked Full Access. It never sees your payment details.

Links

Links in the app, such as the Terms of Use, open in Safari. The app itself makes no network connections except to the App Store for your purchase.

Children

Sourced 608 isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
